01. About

I'm an AI engineer who works close to the people using the software. At Stellar Work I help development teams become agentic, run AI workshops, and build graph knowledge stores into agentic workflows.

Before that I spent almost two years at Embraceable making RAG work in production, from ingestion and anonymization to context pruning, plus n8n agents and a Python SDK. Earlier, at DFKI, I worked on Neo4j graphs and moved projects onto OpenShift.

I've loved computers since childhood: outside I got bored easily, but in front of a monitor time flew by. That hasn't changed.
